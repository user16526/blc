  • Our price = the Price on the bloodycase.com item page = client_items.buyout_price. Buff and Skinport are market references only.
  • Fresh: not in the prizes of the last 5 case-battle boards (#63, #96, #97, #98, #99 running) or the last 5 events (Rabbit Hunt, Major Battle of Cologne, World Case Cup, No Name Resort, Start Season). Any wear of the same skin counts as a repeat. 115 excluded item IDs (table 2). Example rejected: Driver Gloves | Snow Leopard (Start Season prize).
  • Buff: lowest ask and highest buy order from csgotrader's Buff163 feed (2026-10-06). Buff stock and buy-order counts need a Buff login and are not shown; Skinport stock is shown instead, so the stock-rule column is not verified against Buff.
  • Kept by our players = withdrawn (status 3) share of case drops, last 90 days; meaningful only at 30+ drops, which only the #10 pick and its backup have.
  • Price drift = Steam 7-day vs 90-day average (csgotrader); every CS2 pick is within ±8%.
  • Rust: one Rust skin at #8 (Plate Carrier – Black, Skinport stock 27). Stainless Facemask rejected: Skinport stock under 10.
  • #3 sits at −8.1% (inside the tolerance); its backup Bayonet | Tiger Tooth FN is at −3.8% if you want it closer. Our site prices some skins far above the market (AWP | Asiimov FT: ours $252.88 vs Buff ask $96.65), so those were avoided.
