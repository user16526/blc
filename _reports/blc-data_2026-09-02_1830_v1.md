# BLC data: Happy Hour design inputs — window overlap, new-client share, MDE (live DB, 2026-09-02)
Source: DB `bloody` via /mcpb (read-only), `public.deposits` (paid, CDC-deleted excluded, amount > 0),
`public.client_login_records`. Timestamps `timestamp without time zone`, assumed UTC. Money USD.
Purpose: the three pulls owed from the 2026-09-02 design session + the sheriff's finding [4] (power)
for `_reports/happy-hour-test-design-options_2026-09-02_v1.md`.

## Headline
**A 3-week test with a 1/3 split cannot detect a revenue lift below ~130 % (raw) or ~45 % (whales capped
at $500). It CAN detect a ~19 % relative lift in depositor rate (share of logged-in clients who deposit).**
So the primary decision metric must be depositor rate / deposit count; net revenue is a guardrail
(direction and prize cost), not the decision. Everything else in the design survives.

## Q4. Power / MDE (sheriff finding [4], pre-start step P1)
Unit = client. Population = distinct clients with a login in the last 21 days (the logged-in split's
real denominator), outcome = that client's paid deposits over the same 21 days (0 for non-depositors).
MDE at α = 0.05 two-sided, power 0.8 (factor 2.8), per arm = population / 3.

| basis (21 d) | n | mean / client | sd | per arm | MDE (relative) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| revenue, logged-in clients, raw | 25 486 | $3.19 | $98.43 | 8 495 | **133 %** |
| revenue, logged-in clients, capped $500/client | 25 486 | $2.01 | $21.96 | 8 495 | **47 %** |
| revenue, depositors only, raw | 1 321 | $61.90 | $428.26 | 440 | 131 % |
| revenue, depositors only, capped $500 | 1 321 | $39.07 | $88.71 | 440 | 43 % |
| depositor rate (logged-in → ≥ 1 deposit) | 25 486 | 5.01 % | — | 8 495 | **18.7 %** (5.01 → 5.95 %) |
| depositor rate, 6 weeks | ~51 000 | 5.01 % | — | ~17 000 | 13.2 % |
| depositor rate, 2 arms 50/50, 3 weeks | 25 486 | 5.01 % | — | 12 743 | 15.3 % |

Whales dominate: 28 clients ≥ $500 in 21 days hold $30 k of $82 k. Any revenue read must be capped or
trimmed, and even then a 3-week test only sees a ~45 % lift.

## Implications for the design doc
1. Primary metric → **depositor rate and deposits per logged-in client over the full day**; net
   incremental revenue (capped) → guardrail: sign + prize cost, not the decision.
2. G5 (answer at 3 weeks) holds only for the depositor-rate metric, and only for lifts ≥ ~19 %.
   A smaller true effect is invisible; say so before the start, not after.
3. If the owner wants revenue as the decision metric, the run must be ≥ 6 weeks AND whale-capped,
   and even then MDE ≈ 33 %.
4. The 02–05 window carries 5 % of deposits: as a full-day lever it is small by construction;
   its test is mainly a cannibalization read.

## Caveats
- Timestamps assumed UTC; bots/bloggers not excluded; login table may include multiple accounts per person.
- MDE uses a normal approximation on heavy-tailed data (raw revenue numbers are indicative only).
- The logged-in population is 21-day logins, not exactly "clients who will see gate_shown" in the test.
