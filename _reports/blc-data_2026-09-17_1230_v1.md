# BLC deposits drop — Sep 1–16 2026 vs Aug 1–16 2026 vs Sep 1–16 2025
Source: live DB `bloody` via /mcpb, queried 2026-09-17. Excludes `clients.flags & 65` (test / do-not-count).
Windows: `deposits.created` [1st, 17th) — timestamp w/o tz, assumed UTC. Amounts: cents / 100, currency not split.

## Verdict
The cause is not seasonality and not demand. It is payment conversion on the main payment system (`payment_system_id = 82`).
Deposit attempts held steady, but paid deposits fell. Waiting will not fix it; the payment provider needs to be checked.

## Numbers

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-09 1–16 | 6379 | 3176 | 49.8% | 1305 | 64,560 | 20.33 | 721 |

Seasonality check: Aug→Sep 2025 GMV **+9%** (59,363 → 64,560). Aug→Sep 2026 GMV **−16%**.

PS 82 (≈90% of GMV):
| window | attempts | paid rate |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-08 | 4590 | 60.8% |
| 2025-09 | 4956 | 56.6% |
| 2026-08 | 4383 | 57.2% |

PS 82 weekly success (status 2 / (2+3)): wk Aug 3 58% · Aug 10 58% · **Aug 17 51%** · Aug 24 51% · Aug 31 50% · Sep 7 47% · Sep 14 48%.
The step down came in the week of **Aug 17–23**. Success has not recovered since.

Secondary: PS 94 attempts rose from 1012 to 1568 (+55%) at a ~5% success rate. These are likely users retrying there after a PS 82 failure (unverified).

Estimated loss: holding the Aug PS 82 rate on Sep attempts would have given ≈ +370 paid deposits ≈ **+7.5k GMV**, which is ~85% of the 8.8k gap vs August.

## Caveats
- Status meaning (2 = paid, 3 = failed) is inferred from `paid_at`, not from app code. There is no payment-system name table in the replica, so ids are unnamed.
- Mixed currencies are summed as one unit.
- The data cannot show the reason for failures (declines vs abandoned checkout). The provider dashboard or dev logs are needed for that.

## SQL
```sql
-- windows summary
WITH w(label, s, e) AS (VALUES ('2025-09','2025-09-01','2025-09-17'),('2026-08','2026-08-01','2026-08-17'),('2026-09','2026-09-01','2026-09-17')),
d AS (SELECT w.label, dp.id, dp.client_id, dp.amount, dp.paid_at FROM w
  JOIN public.deposits dp ON dp.created >= w.s::timestamp AND dp.created < w.e::timestamp
  JOIN public.clients c ON c.id = dp.client_id
  WHERE dp._ab_cdc_deleted_at IS NULL AND (c.flags & 64) = 0 AND (c.flags & 1) = 0)
SELECT d.label, count(*) attempts, count(*) FILTER (WHERE d.paid_at IS NOT NULL) paid,
  count(DISTINCT d.client_id) FILTER (WHERE d.paid_at IS NOT NULL) depositors,
  sum(d.amount) FILTER (WHERE d.paid_at IS NOT NULL)/100.0 gmv, count(DISTINCT fd.client_id) ftd
FROM d LEFT JOIN analytics.first_deposit fd ON fd.deposit_id = d.id GROUP BY 1;

-- PS 82 weekly status split
SELECT date_trunc('week', dp.created)::date wk, dp.status, count(*)
FROM public.deposits dp JOIN public.clients c ON c.id = dp.client_id
WHERE dp._ab_cdc_deleted_at IS NULL AND (c.flags & 65) = 0 AND dp.payment_system_id = 82
  AND dp.created >= '2026-08-03' AND dp.created < '2026-09-17'
GROUP BY 1,2 ORDER BY 1,2;
```
