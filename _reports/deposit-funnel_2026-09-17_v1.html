BLC deposits · days 1–16 · live DB, 2026-09-17

Where Deposits Leak

September 2026 compared with August 2026 and September 2025, same days (1st–16th). More people visit, sign up and start a deposit than ever. The money is lost at the last step: the payment doesn't go through.

Full funnel

People are counted once per window. An "attempt" is one deposit row; a person can try several times. The database can't see deposit-window opens or checkout page views (GA4/Clarity only), so the funnel starts at "logged in".

New vs returning players

"New" = account created inside the same window. Returning players carry ~80% of GMV and account for most of the loss.

By country

Top 10 deposit countries in Sep 2026, sorted by GMV change vs August. "Start → pay" = people who paid ÷ people who started a deposit. Cells shaded where start → pay fell 10+ pp vs August.

By payment system

The replica has no names for payment systems; ids only. PS 82 carries ~86% of GMV.

PS 82 week by week

Weeks start Monday. The step down begins in the week of Aug 17 and hasn't recovered. The Sep 14 week is partial (Sep 14–16).

Read with care

  • "Paid" = deposits.paid_at is set (inferred, not confirmed in app code). Timestamps are assumed UTC.
  • Status semantics changed: in 2025 ~1,200 PS 82 attempts per window stayed pending; in 2026 almost none do, and they show up as failed. Compare paid counts across years, not failed counts.
  • Test and do-not-count accounts (clients.flags & 65) are excluded from deposits and sign-ups. "Logged in" comes from login records without that filter.
  • "Intended by never-payers" = the largest unpaid amount each never-paying person tried. It's an upper bound on lost GMV, not a forecast.
  • Currencies are summed as one unit; the top countries all deposit in USD. Deposit country source (IP or card) is unverified.
  • All clients have partner_id = 1, so traffic source can't be split here. Use GA4 for that.
